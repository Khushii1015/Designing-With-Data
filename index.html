About this project

This is our group project for CCT360. It asks the weather if it's a good day to hang laundry outside, using live data from Open-Meteo. Clouds, wind, rain, temperature and day or night change the picture, and you can click the clothes to take them off the line.

Made by Varnan Goenka, Yuvika Vohra and Khushboo Gill · CCT360 · Fall 2026
